Clark Player

v1.0.5 · Android 8.0+ · 2.6 MB

Your music, your rules. Clark Player is a fully offline Android music player that puts you in control — from a precision equalizer and real-time visualizer to speed, pitch, and reverb adjustments, all wrapped in a clean interface that feels native to your device.

Kotlin Jetpack Compose Media3/ExoPlayer Audio FX FFT Visualizer
Download APK
100% free. No ads. No trackers. No internet permission. Just music.
Features

Built for music lovers.

01

Library

All your tracks in one place. Search instantly, build playlists, and mark favorites for quick access. No syncing, no accounts — just your music, organized the way you want.

02

Equalizer

5-band or 10-band graphic EQ with built-in presets — Flat, Bass Boost, Vocal, Electronic, and more. Save your own custom presets. Fine-tune every frequency to match your headphones and taste.

03

Bass & Space

Feel the low end with dedicated bass boost, or widen the soundstage with 3D spatial virtualization. Both adjustable in real-time without leaving the player screen.

04

Reverb

Add depth to any track with Small Room, Medium Room, Large Room, or Plate reverb. Like playing in a concert hall from your headphones.

05

Speed & Pitch

Slow down a guitar riff to learn it, speed up a podcast, or shift pitch independently. Range from 0.25x to 4.0x, with zero unwanted distortion when you don't want it.

06

Playback

Loop any section for practice with A-B repeat. Shuffle your queue, repeat a track or entire playlist. Full control over how your music plays.

07

Visualizer

Audio-reactive visuals that move with your music. Choose from bar equalizer, vortex, or video-reactive modes — all rendered in real-time from FFT data.

08

Recorder

Capture ideas, memos, or jam sessions in high-quality .m4a. Built right into the app — no extra download needed.

Screenshots

See it in action.

Navigation

Five tabs — Home, Music, Player, Visuals, Settings. Everything is one tap away. The now-playing screen puts your album art front and center with swipe-up access to the full audio control panel.

Themes & Visualizer

Light and dark themes switch automatically with your system. The visualizer reacts to whatever's playing — bars bounce to the beat, vortex swirls with the rhythm, video mode overlays visuals on your album art.

Inspired by the depth of Music Speed Changer, the energy of Avee Player, and the simplicity of Lark Player — built as something new.

Installation

Safe, simple, fast.

Clark Player should combine the powerful audio-control capabilities of Music Speed Changer, the highly dynamic audio-reactive visualization experience of Avee Player, and the clean, accessible, media-focused UI/UX approach of Lark Player. Clark Player should not copy their interfaces or branding; instead, it should take inspiration from the types of functionality users expect from these apps and build a unique Clark Player identity around them.
