VERTEX Characters

V1.0 · AI CHARACTERS + ROLEPLAY + COMMUNITY

A free AI character platform built to bring characters, stories, and conversations to life. Create your own characters, give them unique personalities, memories, stories, and behaviors, then chat and roleplay with them. Discover characters created by the community, explore trending personalities, publish your own creations, and build immersive conversations — all in one place. Inspired by the idea of Character.AI, VERTEX Characters is being built as a free-to-use platform where anyone can create, discover, and interact with AI characters.
