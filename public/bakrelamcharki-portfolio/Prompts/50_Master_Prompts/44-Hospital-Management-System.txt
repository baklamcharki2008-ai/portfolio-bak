# MASTER PROMPT: Hospital Management System

## Project Overview
Build a comprehensive hospital management system with patient registration, appointments, EHR, prescriptions, billing, pharmacy, lab, radiology, and staff management.

## Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** Next.js 14 + TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Shadcn UI, TanStack Table, Recharts, Zustand, React Query, FullCalendar
- **Backend:** Node.js + Express + TypeScript, tRPC
- **Database:** PostgreSQL + Redis
- **Auth:** NextAuth.js + JWT + RBAC
- **Storage:** S3-compatible (medical records, images, reports)
- **Notifications:** Resend (email), Twilio (SMS)
- **Infrastructure:** Docker, GitHub Actions, Railway

## Detailed Requirements

### User Roles
- Super Admin, Hospital Admin, Doctor, Nurse, Receptionist, Pharmacist, Lab Technician, Radiologist, Accountant, Patient

### Patient Management
- Registration: name, DOB, gender, blood group, address, phone, email, emergency contact, insurance info, medical history summary
- Unique patient ID (MRN - Medical Record Number)
- Patient search: by name, MRN, phone, national ID
- Patient timeline: visits, admissions, surgeries, immunizations, allergies, medications
- Documents upload: ID, insurance card, previous reports, consent forms
- Patient portal: view appointments, medical records, prescriptions, lab results, bills

### Appointment Scheduling
- Calendar view (FullCalendar): doctor schedule, room availability
- Appointment types: consultation, follow-up, emergency, procedure, surgery, telemedicine
- Book appointment: patient search → select doctor → select date/time → confirm
- Walk-in appointment
- Online appointment booking (patient portal)
- Appointment reminders (SMS/email 24h before)
- Appointment status: scheduled, confirmed, checked-in, in-progress, completed, cancelled, no-show
- Cancellation and rescheduling
- Waitlist management

### Electronic Health Records (EHR)
- Medical history: past illnesses, surgeries, hospitalizations, family history, social history
- Allergies: drug, food, environmental (with severity)
- Medications: current, past, discontinued (with dosage, frequency, prescribing doctor)
- Immunizations: vaccine name, date, administered by, next due
- Vitals: BP, heart rate, temperature, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, height, weight, BMI (recorded per visit)
- Clinical notes: SOAP format (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan)
- Diagnosis: ICD-10 codes, primary, secondary
- Procedures: CPT codes, date, surgeon, notes
- Document imaging: X-rays, MRI, CT scans, ultrasound (DICOM viewer simulation)
- Lab results: test name, result, reference range, flag (abnormal/high/low), date
- Growth charts (pediatrics)
- Pregnancy history (OB/GYN)
- Visit history: chronological list of all encounters

### Doctor Dashboard
- Today's appointments list with patient info
- Patient search and quick access
- Consultation workflow: view vitals → review history → examine → add diagnosis → prescribe → order labs → add notes → complete visit
- Prescription writing: drug search (database), dosage, frequency, duration, notes
- Lab order: select tests, send to lab, view results when ready
- Referral to other doctor/department
- Telemedicine: video call integrated (WebRTC)
- Practice analytics: patients seen, revenue generated, common diagnoses

### Prescriptions
- Drug database: name, generic name, brand, manufacturer, strength, form, category, price
- Prescription: drug, dosage, route, frequency (OD, BID, TID, QID, PRN), duration, quantity, refills, instructions, substitutions allowed?
- E-Prescription: digital signature, QR code, print, send to pharmacy
- Prescription history per patient
- Drug interaction checker (basic: check against current medications)
- Antibiotic stewardship (duration warnings)
- Controlled substance tracking

### Billing & Insurance
- Billing items: consultation fee, procedure fee, medicine, lab tests, room charges, misc
- Generate bill: select patient → add charges → apply discounts → insurance claim → generate invoice
- Payment modes: cash, card, UPI, insurance, credit, online
- Partial payment and credit
- Insurance claim processing: verify coverage, submit claim, track status, follow up
- TPA (Third Party Administrator) integration
- Generate receipts: bill receipt, payment receipt, insurance receipt
- Daily/ monthly/ yearly revenue reports
- Outstanding payments report
- Price list management

### Pharmacy Management
- Inventory: drug name, category, manufacturer, supplier, batch no, expiry date, purchase price, selling price, quantity, reorder level
- Purchase order management
- Stock inward: add stock (batch, expiry, mrp)
- Stock outward: issue to patient (against prescription)
- Expiry tracking and alerts
- Low stock alerts
- Pharmacy sales report
- Supplier management
- Drug dispense workflow: pharmacist views prescription → dispense → update inventory

### Lab Management
- Test catalog: test name, code, category, sample type (blood, urine, stool, tissue), price, turnaround time, reference ranges
- Lab order: doctor orders → sample collected → sample received in lab → test performed → result entered → result verified → result published
- Barcode generation for sample tracking
- Automated result entry (connected to analyzers, simulated)
- Manual result entry
- Abnormal flagging (high/low out of reference range)
- Report generation (PDF with letterhead)
- Lab dashboard: pending samples, in-progress tests, completed, quality control

### Radiology
- Imaging modalities: X-ray, MRI, CT, Ultrasound, Mammography, PET scan, DEXA
- Order imaging: doctor orders → schedule → perform → radiologist reads → report
- DICOM viewer (simplified: show radiology images with zoom, pan, window/level)
- Radiology report: findings, impression, recommendation
- Report template management (normal reports with auto-fill)
- Comparison with previous studies

### Ward & Bed Management
- Ward types: General, Semi-Private, Private, ICU, NICU, PICU, CCU, Isolation
- Bed management: bed number, ward, room, status (available, occupied, reserved, maintenance)
- Admission workflow: patient → admission type (emergency/elective) → ward allocation → bed assignment → admission notes
- Discharge workflow: discharge summary → billing clearance → bed release
- Bed occupancy report
- Average length of stay report

### Operation Theatre
- OT scheduling: date, time, surgeon, anesthetist, OT room, procedure
- Pre-op checklist
- Intra-op notes
- Post-op instructions
- OT inventory: equipment, instruments, consumables
- OT utilization report

### Staff Management
- Staff types: doctors, nurses, technicians, admin, support
- Staff profile: photo, name, department, designation, qualification, specialization, experience, license number, contact, emergency contact
- Duty roster (shift scheduling)
- Attendance tracking
- Leave management
- Payroll (salary, deductions, advances)
- Staff performance metrics

### Admin Dashboard
- Hospital overview: total beds, occupied, available, today's appointments, today's admissions, today's discharges, today's revenue, pending lab reports
- Charts: revenue trend, patient volume, bed occupancy, department performance, doctor performance
- Recent activities
- Alerts: low stock, expiring drugs, pending lab reports, insurance claims pending
- System health monitoring

### Implementation Plan

Phase 1: Foundation (Week 1-2) - Auth, roles, patient registration, doctor profiles
Phase 2: Appointments (Week 2-3) - Scheduling, calendar, check-in, notifications
Phase 3: EHR (Week 3-5) - Medical records, vitals, clinical notes, diagnosis, ICD-10
Phase 4: Prescriptions (Week 5-6) - Drug DB, e-prescription, drug interaction, printing
Phase 5: Billing (Week 6-7) - Billing items, invoice, payment, insurance, reports
Phase 6: Pharmacy (Week 7-8) - Inventory, purchase, dispensing, supplier management
Phase 7: Lab & Radiology (Week 8-10) - Test catalog, order workflow, results, DICOM viewer
Phase 8: Ward, OT & Staff (Week 10-11) - Bed management, OT scheduling, staff management
Phase 9: Polish (Week 11-13) - Admin dashboard, reports, patient portal, testing

## Final Instructions
Build a comprehensive hospital management system with patient registration, appointment scheduling, electronic health records (EHR), e-prescriptions, billing/insurance, pharmacy inventory, lab management with results, radiology with DICOM viewer, ward/bed management, operation theatre scheduling, and staff management. The EHR must include SOAP notes, ICD-10 codes, vitals, allergies, immunizations, medications, and visit history. Include a patient portal for viewing records and booking appointments. Support multiple user roles with granular permissions. Design a clean, medical-themed UI with calming blues and accessibility-focused design.
