# MASTER PROMPT: Apple Store Clone

## Project Overview
Build a premium Apple Store clone with product pages, shopping experience, trade-in, financing (Apple Card), customization (configurator), and integration with Apple services.

## Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** Next.js 14 + TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Three.js (3D product viewer), GSAP (scroll animations)
- **Backend:** Node.js + Express + TypeScript
- **Database:** PostgreSQL + Redis
- **Payments:** Stripe + Apple Pay
- **Auth:** Apple ID sign-in + email
- **Storage:** S3-compatible
- **Infrastructure:** Vercel + Cloudflare

## Detailed Requirements

### Apple Design Language
- Minimalist white/gray design with San Francisco typography
- Large product hero images with gradient backgrounds
- Product navigation bar (sticky, product name + buy button)
- Parallax scrolling sections
- Grid system and consistent spacing
- Smooth page transitions

### Product Pages
- Product navigation bar: Overview, Tech Specs, Compare, Buy
- Hero section: product name, tagline, price, CTA
- Product images: gallery with zoom, 360° rotation
- Configurator: color, storage, size, connectivity, accessories
- Price summary with trade-in value
- Finance options: Apple Card Monthly Installments, carrier deals
- Tech specs comparison table
- What's in the box
- Accessories section (cross-sell)
- Customer reviews

### Product Configurator
- Select options: color, storage, size, connectivity
- Visual preview updates on selection (3D model or image swap)
- Price updates in real-time
- Trade-in value estimate
- AppleCare+ add-on
- Accessory add-ons (case, charger, AirPods)
- In-store pickup vs delivery
- Summary sidebar with add to bag

### Shopping Bag & Checkout
- Bag icon with item count (badge animation)
- Bag overlay: item list, price, estimated delivery, edit/remove
- Express checkout: Apple Pay (default)
- Full checkout: guest or sign in
- Delivery options: standard, express, in-store pickup
- Same-day delivery in select areas
- Gift options: gift wrap, gift message, gift receipt
- Order confirmation with delivery tracking

### Apple Trade In
- Device selector (iPhone, iPad, Mac, Watch, Android)
- Device condition assessment (series of questions)
- Trade-in value estimate (real-time)
- How to send device (kit shipping or in-store drop-off)
- Apply trade-in value to new purchase

### Apple Card Financing
- Monthly installment calculator
- Apply for Apple Card (simulated)
- 3% Daily Cash back on purchase
- Financing term options: 6, 12, 18, 24 months
- Interest rate display

### Store & Pickup
- Store locator with map
- In-store availability checker
- Reserve for pickup
- Personal setup appointment booking
- Today at Apple (in-store sessions)

### Account & Services
- Apple ID sign-in
- Order tracking
- Saved payment methods
- Address book
- Recently viewed
- AppleCare+ coverage dashboard
- My Support history

### Admin Dashboard
- Product management (models, colors, storage, pricing)
- Inventory management (by store, warehouse)
- Order management
- Customer management
- Trade-in management
- Promotions management
- Analytics dashboard

### Performance
- 95+ Lighthouse on product pages
- Image optimization (Apple uses HEIC/AVIF)
- SSR for product pages
- Component lazy loading
- Smooth 60fps scroll animations

### Implementation Plan

Phase 1: Design Foundation (Week 1) - Apple design system, typography, colors, components
Phase 2: Product Pages (Week 2-3) - Product hero, gallery, configurator, specs
Phase 3: Configurator (Week 3-4) - Full product builder with real-time pricing
Phase 4: Cart & Checkout (Week 4-5) - Shopping bag, Apple Pay, checkout flow
Phase 5: Trade-In & Financing (Week 5-6) - Trade-in assessment, Apple Card financing
Phase 6: Store & Pickup (Week 6-7) - Store locator, availability, in-store pickup
Phase 7: Account (Week 7-8) - Apple ID, order tracking, saved items
Phase 8: Polish (Week 8-9) - Admin, performance, SEO, testing

## Final Instructions
Build a premium Apple Store clone with Apple's exact design language, product configurator, Apple Pay checkout, trade-in system, and Apple Card financing. Product pages must have the sticky navigation bar with buy button, parallax scrolling, 3D product viewer, and real-time configurator pricing. Checkout must prioritize Apple Pay with guest sign-in. Include trade-in with condition assessment. Design must be pixel-perfect to Apple.com with smooth animations and transitions.
