Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how this personal portfolio site ("the Site"), operated by Shelby__x1, handles information when you visit or interact with it — for example, through the contact form.

This is a personal, non-commercial portfolio. The Site does not sell, rent, or trade any personal information to third parties.

Data Collection

The Site collects information in two limited ways:

  • Contact form: When you submit the contact form, your name, email address, and message are sent directly to the site owner's inbox so they can reply to you. This information is not stored in a database on this Site and is not used for marketing.
  • Local preferences: Your chosen theme (light/dark) and language are saved in your browser's local storage so the Site remembers your preference on your next visit. This data never leaves your device.

Cookies

This Site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. It uses your browser's localStorage — a small storage mechanism similar to a cookie — purely to remember your theme and language preference. You can clear this at any time through your browser settings without affecting your ability to use the Site.

Third-Party Services

The Site loads a small number of external resources to function correctly:

  • Google Fonts — for the site's typography.
  • Font Awesome (cdnjs) — for icons.
  • Contact form delivery service — used only to relay contact form submissions to the site owner's email inbox.

These providers may process technical data such as your IP address as part of normal web delivery, under their own privacy policies. This Site does not control that data once it is requested from their servers.

Security

The Site is served over HTTPS and the contact form includes basic spam-prevention measures. While reasonable care is taken to protect any information submitted, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and this Site cannot guarantee absolute security.

User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask what information (if any) is held about you.
  • Request that any message you sent be deleted from the owner's inbox.
  • Clear your local theme/language preferences at any time via your browser settings.

To exercise any of these rights, use the contact details below.
